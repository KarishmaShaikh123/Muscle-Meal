Choose Your Focus – Burn Fat

Burning fat isn’t just about exercise—it’s about smart eating, steady habits, and the right fuel. Let protein and mindful nutrition speed up your results.

🥗 High-Protein, Low-Carb Meals

Swap refined carbs with high-protein options like lentils, eggs, or grilled chicken to stay full longer and burn more calories.

🔥 Thermogenic Foods

Add fat-burning foods like green tea, chili peppers, black coffee, and ginger to increase metabolism naturally.

🚶‍♀️ Fasted Morning Walk

Doing light cardio on an empty stomach can help tap into fat stores more effectively early in the day.

💧 Stay Hydrated

Water aids digestion and metabolism. Aim for at least 2–3 liters a day, especially before meals.

⏱️ Avoid Late-Night Snacking

Stop eating 2–3 hours before bed to let your body burn fat instead of storing extra calories overnight.

🍳 Don’t Skip Breakfast

A protein-rich breakfast boosts metabolism and prevents overeating later in the day.
