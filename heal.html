Choose Your Focus – Heal & Recover

Healing is just as important as training. Support your body's recovery with the right nutrients, habits, and rest. Here’s how to boost your healing naturally.

🧬 Collagen-Rich Foods

Bone broth, chicken skin, and fish are rich in collagen, helping rebuild tissues, joints, and skin after strain or injury.

🍊 Vitamin C for Repair

Citrus fruits, bell peppers, and strawberries boost immunity and aid in wound healing and collagen formation.

🥩 Quality Protein Intake

Protein supports muscle repair. Include lean meats, tofu, eggs, and lentils to help cells rebuild and regenerate.

💤 Prioritize Deep Sleep

Healing happens during rest. Aim for 7–9 hours of deep, uninterrupted sleep to support hormonal balance and recovery.

🧘 Light Stretching & Breathwork

Gentle movement and breathing exercises improve circulation, reduce inflammation, and calm the nervous system.

🫐 Anti-Inflammatory Foods

Include turmeric, berries, leafy greens, and omega-3s to help reduce internal swelling and speed up healing.
