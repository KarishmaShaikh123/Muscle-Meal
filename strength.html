Choose Your Focus – Build Strength

Want to grow stronger? Protein is your muscle’s best friend. With the right diet and habits, you’ll lift more, last longer, and recover faster.

🍗 Prioritize Protein

Consume lean meats, paneer, tofu, or legumes in every meal to support muscle repair and growth.

🏋️‍♀️ Post-Workout Protein

Take a protein shake within 30 minutes after strength training to maximize muscle recovery and gains.

🥚 Don’t Skip Meals

Eat every 3–4 hours with protein-rich foods to maintain a steady supply of muscle-building nutrients.

🌿 Combine Smartly

Pair carbs (like oats or sweet potatoes) with protein to fuel intense workouts and enhance strength.
